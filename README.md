# Market Day Slides

Year 9 Economics and Business: Trade, Enterprise and Market Day. 16 explainer lessons over 4 weeks, built to go with the student booklet.

Each lesson is four slides:

1. **Intro**: lesson title, today's question and the five keywords
2. **Learn**: the key ideas explained
3. **Watch**: a short YouTube video with three "watch for" prompts (no writing)
4. **Example**: a worked or real-world example with click-to-reveal answers, then "Over to your booklet"

## Using it

- Arrow keys or Back/Next to move. Next on the last slide jumps to the next lesson.
- N shows teacher notes, F goes full screen.
- Click the lesson numbers at the top to jump to any lesson.
- Link straight to a slide with `#L7-4` (Lesson 7, slide 4).
- Videos play when the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opened as a local file, they show as links to YouTube instead.

## Swapping a video

All videos are in the `VIDEOS` list near the top of the script in `index.html`. Replace the `id` with the part after `watch?v=` in any YouTube link. Add `start:90` to start 90 seconds in.
