Step 4 of 7 · Finance leads, everyone checks

Shopping list

List everything you need to make and sell your product. Look up prices on a supermarket or shop website and estimate the cost of each item. The totals add up for you.

Your $10 school budget

The school will buy up to $10 of items for your team (mark them School buys). Anything over that, your team pays for (mark it We'll buy it).

Don't forget the extras: cups, napkins, bags, sticky tape, string, price signs, gloves for food, and anything to decorate the stall. Things you already own still go on the list, marked Already have, but they cost $0.

Test your price: ask 10 people

Ask 10 students outside your team: "Would you pay $0 for our product?" Tap each box: once for yes, twice for no, three times to clear.

Pricing tips

  • Round prices like $1, $2 or $3 are quicker to serve and need less change.
  • Your price must be more than the cost to make one, or you lose money on every sale.
  • Be realistic: most students have only a few dollars at lunch.
  • A deal can sell more: "$2 each or 3 for $5".

How to save your work and keep going on another computer

1Save it

Click 💾 Save my work. A file ending in -saved-work.json goes into your Downloads folder.

2Keep it somewhere safe

Move the file to your OneDrive or Google Drive, or email it to yourself. Every team member can have a copy.

3Open it anywhere

On any computer, open this website, click 📂 Open saved work and choose your file. Everything comes back.

On the same computer, your work also saves by itself as you type. Save a file at the end of every lesson anyway, in case the computer is cleared.
